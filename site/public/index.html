Mirror a leader. Keep every guardrail.

A command-line copier for Papertrade perps. It watches a leader's live wallet stream, opens and closes the same positions in your account with a trading-only session key, and stops at limits you set first. Dry run is the default.

npx papertrade-copytrade leaders

Read this before you copy anything.
  • Papertrade leverage goes to 1000x. A 0.1% adverse move can liquidate a 1000x position and lose its entire margin.
  • Copies open after the leader, at a different price, with slippage and fees. You will not match a leader's results.
  • Only fund the follower account with money you can lose. The guardrails below limit exposure, they do not remove risk.
  • The generated command is a dry run. It signs nothing for submission. Going live is a separate, deliberate step in your terminal.
Who

Your wallet address. Its key stays in your terminal as PAPERTRADE_WALLET_KEY and is never entered here.

How it works

      
    
        
  1. Stream. The leader's wallet feed is read live from Papertrade. Each open and close arrives as an event.
    2. 
        
  2. Plan. A pure planner turns the event into an action: size it, apply the leverage rule, enforce the protocol minimums ($10 margin, $10,000 notional) and every guardrail, or skip it with a logged reason.
    3. 
        
  3. Sign. A session key signs the EIP-712 intent. It can open and close positions and can never withdraw. Your wallet key is used once to register it.
    4. 
        
  4. Remember. Leader-to-follower position mapping is saved to a private file, so a restart picks up exactly where it stopped.
